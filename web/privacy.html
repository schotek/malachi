Privacy

Privacy policy

Malachi Mail runs on your computer. It has no account with us, no advertising, no analytics and no telemetry.

Malachi Mail is developed by Vladislav Janeček. This policy says what the app and this website do with your data. It applies to every edition of the app: the Microsoft Store package, the installers and packages from this site and from GitHub, and builds from the source code.

On your computer

Your mail, contacts, calendars, attachments and settings are stored on your computer, in your user profile. Passwords and sign-in tokens are kept in the system's own store: the Windows Credential Manager, the macOS Keychain or the Secret Service on Linux. Nothing of it is sent to us. Removing an account in the app deletes its stored mail; deleting the app's data folder removes everything.

Services you connect

The app talks directly to the services you add to it, with the credentials you give it: your mail servers (IMAP and SMTP), Microsoft 365 and Outlook.com through Microsoft Graph, Gmail, CalDAV and CardDAV servers, and, if you add them, Jira and eWay-CRM. What these services do with your data is governed by their own privacy policies. Pictures from the internet in a message are loaded only when you allow it, for that message or that sender.

The assistant

The assistant is optional and off until you turn it on and agree to a consent question. When you use it, the messages you ask about are sent to the AI service you chose, Anthropic's Claude or OpenAI's ChatGPT, under your own account with that service and under its terms and privacy policy. When you register the app with Claude Desktop or Claude Code, those apps read your mail through Malachi Mail on your computer, again under your own account. We do not receive any of this.

Checking for a new version

The installers and packages from this site check once a day whether a newer version exists, by asking admin.malachimail.app. The request carries the app's version and nothing else: no identifier, no account, no mail. As with any request on the internet, the server sees your IP address. The check can be turned off in Preferences → General → Updates. The Microsoft Store edition is updated by the Store and does not make this check.

This website

The website uses no cookies, no trackers and no fonts or scripts from other sites. Its two forms, Report a problem with the app and Suggest a feature, send what you fill in (an optional name and e-mail address, the text and, for a report, an optional screenshot, made smaller and stripped of its metadata) together with the page's language and address. A report or a suggestion is kept for 12 months and used only to answer it and to improve the app. The forms are protected against spam by Cloudflare Turnstile, which is loaded only when a form is opened. The site is served by Cloudflare.

Children

Malachi Mail is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page. Questions about it can be sent through Report a problem with the app.
